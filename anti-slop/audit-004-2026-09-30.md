# Approved UI fixes 004

Mode: **AFTER**. This follow-up applies only the two owner-approved findings:
control-boundary contrast and native range-thumb color.

**Design read:** A low-light, touch-first bedside remote for a listener using
a phone or tablet, in the established matte dark olive and warm beige visual
language. Dials: **ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1**.

## Changes

1. **R-25 fixed:** the shared control border is now `#75816f`. Measured
   contrast is **3.68:1** against the `#222821` panel and **3.13:1** against
   the `#2d342b` control surface, above the 3:1 non-text requirement.
2. **R-29 and R-31 fixed:** range inputs now opt out of native browser
   appearance before applying the existing custom WebKit and Firefox thumb
   styles. The thumb uses the established `#e2c89d` warm accent instead of a
   browser-native blue accent.

The brighter olive boundary serves low-vision control discovery without
changing the calm bedside palette. The warm range thumb keeps volume as a
native-feeling control while preserving the interface's single functional
accent.
